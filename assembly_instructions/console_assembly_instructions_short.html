Part 1 — Base Legs Attachment (Under Console)
1
Invert Cabinet & Position 4 Legs
2-Person Lift
  • Place console upside down on a soft rug or blanket.
  • Locate the 4 pre-drilled corner positions on the base.
  • Align the 4 mounting holes of each base leg (A).
Underside of Console (Inverted on Blanket)
2
Secure 4 Legs & Stand Upright
16× Screws (E)
  • Insert and hand-tighten 4 screws (E) into each leg plate.
  • Do not overtighten with power drills.
  • With two people, lift and rotate console upright onto its legs.

3

  • Place live-edge slab (D) upside down on protective pads.
  • Position 3 spindle legs (B) over matching pre-drilled holes (1 leg on narrow end, 2 on wide/forked end).
  • Secure each diamond plate using 4 screws (F).
  • Turn the completed slab assembly upright.

TOP ANCHORING & FINAL SETUP
Part 3 — Anchoring Live-Edge Top to Cabinet
4
Insert Anchor Screws from Inside
3× Screws (G) Upward
  • Open the front cabinet doors and work from the interior ceiling.
  • Insert 3 anchor screws (G) upward through pre-drilled holes.
  • Turn each screw until the tip is just visible (1–2 mm) on the top surface.
Drive Screws (G) Upward
Tip 1–2 mm Exposed
5
Position & Lock Live Edge Top
Tighten 3× Screws (G)
  • Lower the live-edge slab assembly upright onto the console.
  • Align the bottom peg holes of the 3 spindle legs over the exposed screw tips.
  • From inside the cabinet, securely tighten all 3 screws (G) into the spindle legs.
Guide Spindles Over Pins & Hand-Tighten Inside
Part 4 — Final Stability Check & Leveling
6
Level Base & Eliminate Floor Wobble
Buffer Pads (H) as Needed
  • Position the assembled console on a level floor in its final location.
  • Gently rock the unit side-to-side to test for stability.
  • If any wobble occurs due to floor variation, peel and stick an adhesive buffer pad (H) under the floating leg foot.
  • Re-check until the unit sits completely solid and balanced.
