Part 2 — Upright Positioning & Base Leveling
5
Lift Upright (Do Not Drag)
2-Person Lift Required
  • With two people, carefully lift tall cabinet completely off floor.
  • Rotate upright and set squarely onto its four legs.
  • Do not drag to avoid damaging legs or flooring.
2-Person Lift & Rotate Upright
6
Check Wobble & Apply Buffer Pads
Buffer Pads (F) as Needed
  • Place cabinet in final location on a level floor.
  • Gently test for any side-to-side wobble.
  • If uneven, apply adhesive buffer pad (F) beneath floating leg.
Stability Rocking Test
Buffer Pad (F)
Part 3 — Door Handle Assembly
7
Insert Handle Screws from Inside
4× Screws (E)
  • Open tall cabinet doors.
  • Insert 2 handle screws (E) through pre-drilled holes from inside.
  • Drive screws until tips are just visible (1–2 mm) on outside face.
Insert Screws (E) Inside Door
8
Align & Tighten Handles
Fasten Securely
  • Place handle (B) over exposed screw tips on outside.
  • Ensure handle sits flush and aligned against door face.
  • Hold handle firmly and tighten screws from inside. Do not overtighten.
