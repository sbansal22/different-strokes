PART 1: BASE LEGS
1
Invert Console & Position 4 Base Legs
  • Carefully place the console cabinet upside down onto a soft, clean blanket to protect the painted finish.
  • Locate the 4 pre-drilled corner positions on the bottom panel.
  • Place each tapered leg (A) over its corresponding pre-marked location and align the 4 mounting holes.
Requires 2 People
Underside of Console (Inverted on Blanket)
2
Secure All 4 Legs & Stand Upright
  • Insert and hand-tighten all 4 screws (E) into each leg plate. Repeat for all four legs (16 screws total).
  • Do not overtighten with power tools to avoid stripping the wood.
  • With two people, lift and rotate the console upright onto its four base legs.
16× Screws (E) — 4 per Leg
4× Screws (E) Per Leg
2-Person Upright Turn

PART 2: LIVE-EDGE RISERS
3
Attach Three Spindle Legs to Live-Edge Slab
  • Take the live-edge wood top (D) and carefully place it upside down on a padded surface.
  • Place the 3 turned spindle legs (B) over the corresponding pre-drilled positions on the underside (1 leg on the narrow end, 2 legs on the wider/forked end).
  • Align the 4 holes on each diamond plate and tighten all screws (F) securely (4 per leg, 12 total).
  • Once secured, turn the live-edge assembly upright again.
12× Screws (F) — 4 per Spindle Plate
Underside of Live-Edge Slab (Resting on Soft Cushions)
Fasten 4× Screws (F) Per Plate

PART 3: TOP ANCHORING
4
Insert Anchor Screws from Inside Cabinet
  • Open the console front doors and work from the interior ceiling.
  • Insert the 3 anchor screws (G) upward through the pre-drilled ceiling holes.
  • Turn each screw until the tip is just visible (1–2 mm) on the top surface to act as guide pins.
3× Anchor Screws (G)
Drive Screws (G) Upward Inside Cabinet
Screw Tip Exposed (1–2 mm)
5
Position & Secure Live Edge Top
  • Carefully lower the live-edge slab with its 3 attached spindle legs onto the top of the console.
  • Align the bottom center hole of each spindle leg directly over the exposed screw tips.
  • Hold each leg firmly and hand-tighten the 3 screws (G) fully from inside the cabinet until securely locked.
Lock 3× Screws (G) from Inside
Lower Slab Onto Pins & Tighten From Inside

PART 4: LEVELING & VERIFICATION
6
Check for Stability & Level the Base
  • Place the assembled console in its final location on a level floor.
  • Gently test for any wobbling or floor unevenness.
  • If uneven, apply an adhesive buffer pad (H) underneath the floating base leg until the console rests flat and solid.
